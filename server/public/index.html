Grounded in Research

Scoring Model & Literature Survey

Directly mapping findings from modern browser security research into automated risk scoring.

Tiered Permission Weights (1-10)

Weights are proportional to blast radius. Critical capabilities (debugger, proxy, nativeMessaging) score 9–10, while isolated utilities (storage, alarms) sit at 1.

Runtime Network Attribution

Grounded in Singh et al. (2025): extensions with minimal permissions can still siphon user data. We observe background network calls and penalize unfamiliar destination domains.

Silent Permission Drift Detection

An extension audited as benign on day 1 can be updated months later with broad permissions. SentinelAudit records timestamped historical scans to spotlight delta increases.

Plain-Language Diagnostics

No cryptic permission names: security teams and users see actionable descriptions like "can inspect active network traffic" and "can read cookies for all visited sites".

Host Grant Normalization

An <all_urls> grant cannot be diluted by padding narrow hostnames, and dozens of specific URLs cannot out-weight an overarching wildcard access rule.

Local-First PostgreSQL Storage

Zero sensitive data leaks to third parties. Audit trails and domain observations are persisted in local PostgreSQL (or embedded PGlite WASM) under your enterprise control.
